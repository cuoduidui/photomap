## English (en)

**App name:** PhotoMap

**Short description (100 chars max):**
Turn your photo album into an interactive travel map with AI-generated travel journals.

**Full description:**
PhotoMap turns a folder of photos into an interactive map of your travels.

- Photo map: Photos with GPS are automatically placed on a map. Zoom in, filter by date or location, and replay your journey as a route.
- Location tree: Browse photos by country, region, city, street and address. Click any place and the map moves there instantly.
- AI travel journal: One click generates a beautiful illustrated travel journal from your photos, with people, places and cameras identified. Available in 7 languages.
- Timeline: Drag a time range and see exactly where you were each day.
- Albums, slideshows and videos: Create themed albums, export photo collages and generate MP4 slideshows with music and narration.
- 100% local and private: Your photos stay on your device. No account, no uploads.

Search terms: travel, map, photo album, GPS, journal, trip, memory, slideshow
